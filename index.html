Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take?

Most orders are ready within 24 hours. Dry cleaning and large items may take up to 48 hours. We'll confirm the timing when you book.

Do you offer free pickup and delivery?

Yes. We collect and return your items at no extra cost within our service area.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently serve the main neighbourhoods of Nairobi. Contact us on WhatsApp to check whether your location is covered.

How do I pay?

You can pay by M-Pesa, cash on delivery, or card. Payment details are shared when you confirm your booking.

What if I'm not home at pickup time?

Leave clear instructions with the guard or a neighbour, or message us to reschedule. We'll always confirm before collecting.

Do you handle delicate or special fabrics?

Yes. Silk, wool, leather, and beaded pieces get individual care. Mention the fabric when you book so we can treat it properly.

What if a stain doesn't come out?

We treat every stain before washing. Some marks can't be fully removed, and we'll always tell you honestly before we finish.

What if an item gets damaged or lost?

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll investigate and make it right, whether through repair or compensation.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes, you can cancel or change your booking free of charge up to 2 hours before pickup.
